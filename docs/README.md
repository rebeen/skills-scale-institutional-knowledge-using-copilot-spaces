## OctoAcme Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a structured five-phase lifecycle designed to maximize customer value, minimize risk, and maintain team clarity throughout execution:

1. **Initiation** — Validate business need and measurable outcomes, align stakeholders, confirm decision to proceed
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments, define acceptance criteria, identify dependencies and risks
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate toward delivery with consistent quality standards
4. **Release** — Deploy to production with confidence, verify success, and communicate outcomes
5. **Close & Retrospective** — Capture learnings, document action items, and drive continuous improvement

## Core Roles

### Project Manager (PM)
Coordinates delivery, manages schedules, risks, and communications. Responsible for timeline adherence, escalation management, and stakeholder alignment.

### Product Manager (PdM)
Defines desired outcomes, prioritizes the backlog, measures success metrics, and ensures customer value is delivered.

### Developers
Implement features and fixes, collaborate on design and technical approach, maintain code quality and testability.

### QA/Testing
Validate quality against acceptance criteria, execute test plans, verify functionality before release.

### Stakeholders
Provide business context, approvals, strategic direction, and feedback throughout the project lifecycle.

## Key Principles

- **Customer-first** — Prioritize customer value and usability in all decisions
- **Iterative delivery** — Deliver small, testable increments frequently rather than large releases
- **Clear ownership** — Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- **Data-informed** — Measure impact and iterate based on evidence, not assumptions
- **Psychological safety** — Encourage feedback, experimentation, and learning from failures

## Key Artifacts

- **Project Charter / One-pager** — Problem statement, objectives, success metrics, stakeholders, timeline
- **Roadmap & Release Plan** — High-level direction and key milestones
- **Sprint/Iteration Backlog** — Prioritized work with clear acceptance criteria
- **Definition of Done (DoD)** — Explicit quality and completeness standards
- **Risk Register** — Tracked risks with mitigation plans and owners
- **Project Board** — Visual workflow (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done)
- **Retrospective Notes** — Learnings and action items for continuous improvement

## Communication Cadence

- **Daily standups** — 15 minutes; focus on progress, blockers, dependencies
- **Weekly PM/PdM sync** — Alignment between Project Manager and Product Manager
- **Twice-weekly delivery standups** — Team execution updates (or as agreed)
- **Weekly stakeholder updates** — Status, risks, decisions needed
- **Sprint/Milestone reviews** — Demo progress, gather feedback
- **Retrospectives** — Post-sprint or post-release learnings and action items
- **Ad-hoc escalations** — When blockers or decisions cannot wait

## Getting Started

1. **For a new project:** Start with [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) to validate the business case and create your one-pager.

2. **For planning:** Use [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) to break work into increments and build your backlog.

3. **During execution:** Reference [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) for day-to-day guidance and [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) for handling dependencies.

4. **Before release:** Follow [Release & Deployment Guide](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) to ensure quality and reduce risk.

5. **After completion:** Run a retrospective using [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) to capture learnings.
