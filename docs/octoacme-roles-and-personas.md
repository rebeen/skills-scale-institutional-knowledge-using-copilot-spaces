# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead / QA Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and execute testing strategies, ensure quality standards are met, and validate that features meet acceptance criteria before release. They collaborate with developers and product teams to build quality into the delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and acceptance criteria validation approach
- Execute and oversee manual and automated testing
- Identify quality gaps and propose improvements
- Participate in sprint planning to ensure testability of features
- Manage regression testing and quality metrics
- Work with developers to ensure acceptance criteria are testable before development begins

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet customer expectations
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Build testing into the development workflow
- Ensure comprehensive test coverage and documentation

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and retrospectives
- Daily standup updates on test status
- Test reports and quality dashboards
- Issue tracking and defect prioritization
- Collaboration with developers on acceptance criteria clarity

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Partner on testability requirements and quality standards; review test coverage
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria interpretation and test scenarios
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics and testing progress in status updates

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate sprint ceremonies, remove impediments, coach the team on Agile practices, and maintain process discipline. They serve as servant leaders who enable the team to deliver value efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on Agile principles and practices
- Maintain the backlog and sprint board
- Help the team self-organize and improve their delivery process
- Escalate systemic issues that impact team velocity

### Goals
- Enable consistent, predictable delivery cadence
- Build team capability and self-management
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Remove organizational barriers to team success

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies (standups, planning, reviews, retros)
- 1-on-1 coaching with team members
- Stakeholder updates on team health and velocity
- Process improvement recommendations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Coach on Agile practices; facilitate ceremonies; remove blockers
- **Product Managers**: Support backlog refinement and prioritization discipline
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on sprint planning and timeline management
- **QA**: Ensure testing is incorporated into sprint planning

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Owner

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architecture decisions, ensure code quality and maintainability, manage technical debt, and mentor the development team on technical best practices. They balance feature velocity with long-term system health.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and major decisions
- Establish and maintain coding standards and architectural patterns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Lead technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Mentor developers on technical skills and best practices
- Manage technical debt and prioritize refactoring work
- Advocate for scalability, security, and maintainability

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Balance feature velocity with technical quality
- Build team capability and knowledge
- Reduce long-term technical risk and complexity

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review feedback and design guidance
- Technical risk assessment in planning meetings
- Mentoring and 1-on-1 technical guidance
- Technical documentation and standards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Mentor and guide technical decisions; review code for patterns and quality
- **Project Managers**: Advise on technical risks and architectural complexity in estimates
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on trade-offs between features and technical debt
- **QA**: Discuss testability and quality implications of architectural decisions

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads drive user experience strategy, own design standards, collaborate on user research, and ensure accessibility and usability across the product. They advocate for the user in all decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define UX strategy and design standards
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility and compliance with design standards
- Collaborate with developers on implementation fidelity
- Iterate on designs based on user feedback and metrics
- Maintain design system and component library

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Ensure user research informs product decisions
- Build consistency and coherence across the product
- Reduce support burden through clear, usable design

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- User research findings and insights
- Design specifications and handoff to development
- Collaboration on accessibility and usability concerns
- Product roadmap input based on user needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Product Managers**: Partner on user research; validate solutions against user needs
- **Developers**: Collaborate on implementation fidelity and technical feasibility
- **QA**: Define usability and accessibility acceptance criteria
- **Project Managers**: Communicate design complexity and review timelines

---

## DevOps / Infrastructure Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers manage deployment infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, monitoring, and production reliability. They enable teams to deploy frequently and safely while maintaining system health.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage deployment infrastructure and cloud resources
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Manage production deployments and rollbacks
- Ensure security, compliance, and disaster recovery
- Optimize infrastructure performance and cost
- Support incident response and post-mortems

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, and reliable deployments
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce time to detect and recover from incidents
- Automate manual operations and reduce toil

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment coordination
- Infrastructure and performance metrics
- Incident response and post-incident reviews
- CI/CD pipeline status and improvements
- Security and compliance updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on deployment strategies and CI/CD pipeline setup
- **Project Managers**: Provide deployment readiness status and risk assessment
- **QA**: Coordinate on test environments and deployment verification
- **Technical Lead**: Advise on infrastructure architecture and scalability

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, approve scope and budget, escalate blockers, and champion projects at the leadership level. They ensure projects align with business strategy and have adequate resources.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success criteria
- Approve project charter and budget
- Provide strategic direction and business context
- Escalate organizational blockers
- Make scope and priority trade-off decisions
- Monitor project progress against business metrics
- Champion the project and secure stakeholder buy-in

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Align project with organizational strategy
- Remove organizational barriers to success
- Make timely decisions to maintain momentum

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and business reviews
- Decision forums for scope and priority changes
- Executive briefings and status reports
- Escalation forums for business-level blockers

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Receive status updates; provide decisions and escalation support
- **Product Managers**: Align on success metrics and business objectives
- **Developers**: Occasional visibility into progress; acknowledge delivery milestones
- **All Roles**: Provide strategic context and remove organizational barriers
